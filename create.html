A quick linguistics lesson

What is a phoneme?

A phoneme is the smallest sound unit that can change meaning in a language. In English, the sounds /p/ and /b/ make pat and bat different words.

Languages do not all use the same sounds. Your language can borrow familiar sounds, invent a new combination, or leave some out entirely.
