Show who is doing the action.

Some languages use one verb form for every subject. Others change the verb to match who is acting. Decide how your language works, add words for I, you, he, she, they, and we, and—if you chose yes—create a marker for each person using the same prefix, suffix, or separate-word placement you used for tenses.

Did you know?

American Sign Language uses the same sign for both 'he' and 'she', but splits 'we' into two separate pronouns with different meanings.

How English handles person

With the verb wait, English only changes the form for he and she (waits). The rest stay wait:

Iwait
youwait
he / shewaits
theywait
wewait
