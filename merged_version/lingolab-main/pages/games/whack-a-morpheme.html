Whack the meaning!

Morphology is the study of how words are formed. Words are built from smaller parts called morphemes, and each part carries meaning.

Example: un + kind + ness = not + caring + state of.

A word sits at the bottom. Whack the alien wearing the meaning of the glowing part, one part at a time. Wrong meaning = lose a heart. Tap, click, or press keys 1–9.
