Give your verbs a sense of time.

Tense tells us when something happens: right now, already, or soon. Below you’ll see how English does it, build tense markers for your language, and invent words for a few basic verbs. If you leave a marker box empty, that tense just uses your base verb—nothing extra added.

Did you know?

In Albanian there are seven different time tenses, plus 6 different additional tenses to express different moods.

How English handles tense

English often adds a small piece before or after the verb to show time. Here’s wait in four tenses:

Infinitiveto wait
Presentwait
Pastwaited
Futurewill wait

Pick a marker and its placement.

For each tense, type a marker (an extra sound or word) and choose where it goes: before the verb, after it, stuck on as a prefix or suffix, or as its own word nearby. Leave the marker box empty if you want that tense to use the base verb only—like English wait in the present, with nothing glued on.
