Make every verb feel personal.

Personal conjugations are verb changes that tell us who is doing an action. Some languages use the same verb for everyone, while others change the verb depending on whether the subject is “I,” “you,” or “they.”

Would you like your verbs to have personal conjugations? Choose yes to create a marker for each person using the same prefix, suffix, or separate-word system from the Tenses step.
