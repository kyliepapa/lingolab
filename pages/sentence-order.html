Choose your sentence order.

A subject is who or what a sentence is about. A verb is the action or state. An object is who or what receives that action.

Sentence order is the pattern your language uses to arrange these three building blocks. Now let’s break it down!

The building blocks

Subject. Verb. Object.

S

Subject

Who or what the sentence is about.

V

Verb

The action or state of being.

O

Object

Who or what receives the action.
