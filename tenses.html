Give your verbs a sense of time.

Tense tells us when an action happens: now, before now, or after now. Languages show tense in different ways, often by changing the verb or adding a marker.

Let’s get into it! For your language, choose a marker and decide where it belongs for each tense. A marker can come before a verb, after it, or stand on its own.

How English handles tense

4 tenses

We’ll start with four useful tenses. In English, the infinitive uses to, while the past often adds -ed to the end of a regular verb.
